# VNTechies course social thumbnails

All assets are 1200 × 630 PNGs, generated with built-in imagegen using the VNTechies logo as a reference.

The course introduction and its lessons share the corresponding thumbnail through `data/courseSocialImages.js`. The catalog and registration page use the updated catalog thumbnail.

The generated thumbnails also represent courses on the website: course catalog cards, homepage course previews, related course cards, and course introduction images. Course introduction frontmatter points to the matching generated asset.
